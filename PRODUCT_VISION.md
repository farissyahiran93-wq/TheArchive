# Product vision — THE ARCHIVE

**Tagline:** Every record has a story. Someone is changing the truth.

An Android-first detective thriller played through a fictional smartphone. The player is an ordinary Greyhaven civilian who notices a discrepancy between two versions of a historical document about a 2019 evacuation. A submitted report leads to a believable reply from Maya Chen. Daniel Mercer is missing. The story grows through people, records, communication, and deductions rather than a stream of exposition.

## Experience goals

1. Make records feel human: each discovery changes what the player understands about people and consequences.
2. Protect agency: choosing text is separate from sending it; evidence is private until the player shares it.
3. Keep uncertainty honest: distinguish a document, an inference, and a verified fact.
4. Let relationships have independent trust, affinity, suspicion, and optional romance paths without making romance necessary for the mystery.
5. Use responsive, readable phone UI with accessible touch targets and motion controls.

## Product envelope

- Season 1: proposed 10 long episodes, subject to story and playtest approval.
- Target duration: 2.5–4 hours of meaningful gameplay per episode, **unverified** until content and playtesting exist.
- Platform: browser development and testing first; Capacitor-ready structure for later Android packaging.
- Phase 00: technical foundation and non-canonical test fixture only. No complete episode or APK.

Success requires playtests of comprehension, pacing, choice clarity, save reliability, accessibility, and emotional impact. A code-complete app alone cannot establish a 10/10 game.
