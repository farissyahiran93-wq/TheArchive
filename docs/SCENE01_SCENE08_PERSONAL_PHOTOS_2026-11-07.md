# Personal photo conversations — Scenes 1 and 8

These are additions to the existing complete scene scripts. Each player line below offers three alternatives; only the selected line is sent, with its corresponding response. The scene's other dialogue and choices remain in their existing screenplays and content files.

## Scene 1 — A Message from Sophie

After Sophie explains Daniel is her brother, she sends a framed family photograph of the two of them.

**Sophie:** This is a family photo of Daniel and me. Maybe seeing his face will jog your memory. It's okay if it doesn't.

**Player — choose one:**

1. “I'm sorry, Sophie. I don't recognise him.”  
   **Sophie:** “Thank you for checking. I didn't want you to feel pressured to remember.”
2. “Is Daniel on the left? He doesn't look familiar to me.”  
   **Sophie:** “Yes, that's Daniel. Thank you for looking, even if it doesn't help.”
3. “I don't know him, but I can see this photo means a lot to you.”  
   **Sophie:** “It does. He's my brother. I'm grateful you took a moment to look.”

The optional reply requires an explicit Send. The image appears in newly started master Scene 1 saves; already-started saves keep their delivered history. Neither the photograph nor the choices invents a prior relationship between Daniel and the player.

## Scene 8 — The Last Check-In

Previously Lucas sent no photo in this scene. Maya's cropped message remains the actual investigation lead. Once the player has progressed in Maya's conversation and Lucas has left the group, a separate optional private conversation becomes available. Lucas does not rejoin the group.

**Lucas:** I saw you in FIND DANIEL before I left. I'm still looking for him, but I'm not ready to share his private messages.

**Player — choose one:**

1. “I know leaving the group didn't mean you stopped caring.”  
   **Lucas:** “Thank you. I needed space, but I never stopped looking for him.”
2. “I understand you want Daniel's private messages kept private.”  
   **Lucas:** “I do. I can talk about what I choose to share, not hand over his conversations.”
3. “We don't have to discuss anything you're not ready to share.”  
   **Lucas:** “I appreciate that. I want to help, but I need to set my own limits.”

**Player — choose one:**

1. “What was Daniel like when it was just the two of you?”  
   **Lucas:** “He could make a completely ordinary afternoon feel like an adventure.”
2. “Do you have a memory of him you would be comfortable sharing?”  
   **Lucas:** “There's an old picture of us I don't mind showing you. It isn't part of his investigation.”
3. “Only if you want to: is there something ordinary you remember about him?”  
   **Lucas:** “He always had a way of finding time for a friend, even when he was busy.”

**Lucas sends a photo:** “That's Daniel and me. It's an old photo; I can't place the exact date. Please keep it between us.”

**Player — choose one:**

1. “Thank you for trusting me with that. I'll keep it private.”  
   **Lucas:** “Thank you. I wanted you to see my friend, not just a missing person's name.”
2. “Do you remember when the photo was taken?”  
   **Lucas:** “Not precisely. It's an old memory, not a way to establish where he was recently.”
3. “I won't share this photo without asking you first.”  
   **Lucas:** “I appreciate that. Please don't pass it around without asking me.”

The generated images use the owner's character portraits as visual references. They are fictional snapshots: the Lucas photo has no established date or location and does not prove the check-in or any investigative claim. Neither image is automatically shared with other characters.
