# Chapter 1 Scene 4 — implementation report

Scene 4 is a separately persisted new-campaign flow. After the actual `TA-S03-SCENE04-UNLOCKED` event and Scene 3 completion, returning to Home records the scoped Sophie-to-Eli enquiry and delivers exactly one Unknown message. It does not open the chat. The enquiry records only that Sophie contacted the owner of an unnamed shared-folder entry and invited them to help; it does not disclose the player's private messages or claim the player accepted. Unknown's identity and portrait remain anonymous to the player.

The [implemented screenplay](THEARCHIVE_CH1_SCENE4_SCREENPLAY.md) derives from `src/content/masterScene04.ts`: ten decisions, three different player choices and corresponding Unknown responses each. The player must select and Send; the sent line is saved before a separately persisted 900 ms Unknown reply. Closing or reloading cannot invent an extra response or duplicate delivery.

`src/game/masterScene04.ts` owns the state transitions and validation. The fictional `DNL_PHONE_ARCHIVE.arc` offer remains encrypted and unverified. Inspecting metadata records an inspection but does not accept the file or expose its contents. Accepting, deferring, declining and reconsidering are separate player actions. Both actual acceptance and inspection are required to record `TA-S04-COMPLETE`, store the source-limited evidence and unlock `TA-S04-SCENE05-UNLOCKED`. Inspection may occur before or after acceptance; the second real action completes the scene once. Scene 5 remains unavailable and recovery remains 0%; TRACE is not installed by this flow.

`src/ui/MasterUnknownConversation.tsx` uses the established chat and modal styles for the attachment and metadata. `src/ui/Messenger.tsx` and `src/game/phonePresentation.ts` integrate the new thread and notification without changing legacy Unknown threads. `src/App.tsx` starts the incoming message on the new route only when the player is on Home; `src/game/store.ts` and its existing scheduler persist and deliver pending replies. `src/game/sceneLibrary.ts` and `src/game/guidance.ts` project actual progress. `src/game/engine.ts` validates new saved state; untagged old saves retain their existing graphs and data.

`src/game/masterScene04.test.ts` exercises all thirty response alternatives, reloaded drafts and pending replies, offer/defer/decline/reconsider, separate inspect/accept paths and legacy-safe gates. `tests/e2e/master-scene04.spec.ts` drives a fresh game through Scenes 1–3, manually opens Unknown after returning Home, and verifies the locked attachment flow after reload. Typecheck, lint and build passed; **1,483 unit tests** and **2 focused browser tests** passed. The full browser run had **196 passed and 1 timeout**; the timed-out legacy Version History test passed on an isolated rerun. The full run was already in progress when final Scene 4 refinements were made, so it is not represented as a clean full regression of the final version. Log: `artifacts/master-story-2026-11-05/scene4-browser.log`.

Known limits: no real encrypted bytes, download or phone access are produced; this is a fictional in-game artifact. Scene 5 and actual recovery are future work. Human pacing/voice review and physical-device acceptance are still outstanding. No APK is built by this task.
