# Chapter 1 Scene 4 — The Unknown Sender

Unknown opens with: **“I know you’re looking for Daniel.”**

Each player line requires a selected reply and Send. Branches rejoin after the matching response.

## Decision 01

**Player A:** Who is this?

**Unknown:** Someone who knows Daniel. I'm not ready to give you my name.

**Player B:** How do you know that?

**Unknown:** Sophie contacted me about a number she had found. She said she had asked its owner to help.

**Player C:** You need to tell me why you are messaging me.

**Unknown:** That's fair. I want to pass on something that may help find him.

## Decision 02

**Player A:** How did you get my number?

**Unknown:** Sophie asked me about the number. I also checked it against a record I could access.

**Player B:** Did Sophie give you my number?

**Unknown:** She asked me whether I recognised the number. I used that number to contact you.

**Player C:** Have you been watching me?

**Unknown:** No. I haven't seen your conversations or your location. Sophie told me she had contacted you.

## Decision 03

**Player A:** What do you know about Daniel?

**Unknown:** I knew him before he disappeared. I don't know where he is now.

**Player B:** How do you know him?

**Unknown:** We knew each other through work. I can't say more without identifying myself.

**Player C:** Why should I believe you know him?

**Unknown:** You shouldn't accept that just because I say it. I can tell you what I have, and you can decide what it proves.

## Decision 04

**Player A:** What do you want from me?

**Unknown:** I want someone to examine a copy of data that might contain a useful lead.

**Player B:** Why contact me at all?

**Unknown:** Sophie said she had reached out to you. I thought you should know there was another source to consider.

**Player C:** I have not agreed to do anything for you.

**Unknown:** I understand. You can hear me out without agreeing to take anything.

## Decision 05

**Player A:** Why not tell Sophie?

**Unknown:** I don't want to put more on Sophie without understanding what this copy contains.

**Player B:** Why not go straight to the police?

**Unknown:** I'm afraid of exposing my own involvement. That isn't a good excuse, and it doesn't mean you shouldn't contact them.

**Player C:** Are you accusing anyone of being unsafe?

**Unknown:** No. I'm being careful because I'm afraid of exposure. I can't tell you who is safe or unsafe.

## Decision 06

**Player A:** What exactly do you have?

**Unknown:** An encrypted archive. I believe it contains a copy of data from Daniel's phone.

**Player B:** Is this something Daniel gave you?

**Unknown:** Daniel asked me to preserve a copy. I'm telling you that; I can't prove the whole chain just by saying it.

**Player C:** Are you offering me a file?

**Unknown:** Yes. An encrypted archive associated with his phone. You can inspect the offer before accepting it.

## Decision 07

**Player A:** How do you know it belongs to him?

**Unknown:** I received it as Daniel's data, but the file alone doesn't independently authenticate every record.

**Player B:** Is the copy complete?

**Unknown:** I can't promise that. The available manifest doesn't tell me everything that might be missing.

**Player C:** Could the archive have been changed?

**Unknown:** It could have been. I haven't independently verified every part of the copy.

## Decision 08

**Player A:** Why is it encrypted?

**Unknown:** It was protected before I received it. The archive is locked; I won't pretend that answers who has handled it.

**Player B:** Can it tell us where Daniel is?

**Unknown:** I don't know. A file like this isn't a live location, and I don't know where he is.

**Player C:** What can I actually see now?

**Unknown:** The attachment information and the limited manifest. The contents remain locked.

## Decision 09

**Player A:** I want to check the source before deciding.

**Unknown:** You should. My claims and the file's metadata are not the same thing.

**Player B:** Do not access my phone or share anything I do.

**Unknown:** I won't ask for access to your contacts or location. You decide whether to accept the file.

**Player C:** I'm willing to look, but I don't trust you yet.

**Unknown:** That's reasonable. Looking at an offer doesn't require you to trust me.

## Decision 10

**Player A:** Offer the file. I will inspect it first.

**Unknown:** All right. The attachment is still locked. Inspect it and decide for yourself.

**Player B:** Show me what you can without opening it.

**Unknown:** I can offer the encrypted archive and its limited metadata. Nothing is decrypted by looking.

**Player C:** I may decline, but I want to see the offer.

**Unknown:** That's your choice. You can leave it and come back later.

## Encrypted archive

Unknown offers **DNL_PHONE_ARCHIVE.arc**. The player can inspect the metadata, defer, decline for now, reconsider or explicitly accept. Inspection and acceptance are separate actions. The archive remains locked; no phone records are recovered. Both inspection and acceptance are required before Scene 5 unlocks.
