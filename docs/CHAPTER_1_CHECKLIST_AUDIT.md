# Chapter 1 progression audit

The audit follows the ten-scene master Chapter 1 route, including earlier saves on that route. The separately retained article and bulletin routes are historical editions. Their authored progression is outside this refactor.

| Scene or system | Existing gate and location | Classification | Finding and replacement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · A Message from Sophie | `scene01.ts`, `sendScene01Choice`, `scene01Invitation` | KEEP | Manual replies and group membership are actual narrative decisions. Reading notifications changes the inbox only. |
| 2 · The Uninvited Guest | `scene01.ts`, `beginScene02`, group completion and DM handoff | KEEP | Lucas's departure and Sophie's delivered invitation are story events. Opening the DM starts the next conversation. |
| 3 · Just Between Us | `scene01.ts`, `openScene03`, private completion | KEEP | Completion follows the delivered end of Sophie's conversation. The chat visibility flag controls message delivery. |
| 4 · The Unknown Sender | `masterScene04.ts`, `masterScene04Action`, `validateMasterScene04`; `masterScene05.ts`, `start` | REMOVE | Inspecting a preview was a second requirement after Proceed. A real Proceed decision now completes Scene 4. Scene 5 remains manual and details are optional. |
| 5 · The Digital Archive | `masterScene05.ts`, archive and installation gates, `canSaveFragmentFinding`, `readyToDiscuss`; `MasterTrace.tsx` | REWORK | Archive and install disclosures stay visible at the decision itself. Accept, Install and Start Recovery perform distinct actions. A cited fragment observation or comparison supports a saved finding. Opening every fragment is insufficient. |
| 6 · The Missing Hour | `masterScene06.ts`, `timelineReady`, `canDocumentMissingHour`, report controls; `MasterTimeline.tsx` | REPLACE | The old route required all log entries, exact ordering, three acknowledgements and a report review. Comparing the bounding records documents the unresolved interval. A working timeline gives another way to use the same sources. The player still chooses what to say to Unknown. |
| 7 · The Starred Number | `masterScene07.ts`, `record-assessment`, `finish`; `MasterScene07Evidence.tsx` | REWORK | Corroborating a detail or recording an uncertainty matters. Saving that assessment delivers the established next lead without a separate Finish checkpoint. Recipient review remains for optional disclosure. |
| 8 · The Last Check-In | `masterScene08.ts`, `record-assessment`, `availableCaptureAssessments` | KEEP | The crop and available testimony can support a limited assessment. Opening the crop makes it accessible but does not unlock the next scene. |
| 9 · The Secondary Card | `masterScene09.ts`, `canReplyInScene09`, `record-assessment`; `MasterScene09Evidence.tsx` | REWORK | Zoom and code highlighting help the player examine the actual photo. Comparing the reference with an earlier source and saving a supported limitation can progress without a separate highlight receipt. |
| 10 · The Inventory Sheet | `masterScene10.ts`, `compare`, `save-theory`, legacy inventory | REWORK | Keep the actual code search, matching source comparison, and private evidence-backed theory. Existing historical inventory saves can compare without separate metadata and limitation acknowledgements. The call remains gated by the theory or a valid older conclusion. |
| TRACE, Case File and Investigation Board | `MasterTrace.tsx`, `MasterCaseFile.tsx`, `masterCaseFile.ts` | KEEP / REWORK | Availability, provenance, private notes and links persist. Source search, inline evidence selection and three optional contextual hint levels replace opened-file badges. No required count of questions or links. |
| Save and replay | `engine.ts`, `sceneLibrary.ts`, `store.ts`, `MainMenu.tsx` | KEEP | A replay uses a recorded checkpoint. Replacing the main save requires explicit confirmation. Existing findings, legitimate consent and completed scene receipts remain valid. |
