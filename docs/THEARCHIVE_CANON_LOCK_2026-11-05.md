# TheArchive — canon lock and implementation readiness

**Owner decisions · 5 November 2026 · SPOILER / DEVELOPER ONLY.** This document controls the starred-number continuity where older proposed chains conflict. It records future story canon and production assumptions, not implemented gameplay. Five chapters, ten scenes each and TRACE's 20/40/60/80/100% chapter targets remain fixed.

## Locked narrative decisions

1. Rowan misuses administrative access to the internal Greyhaven Harbor Renewal public-enquiry submission system to obtain the fictional player's number. It was not publicly exposed. The player had made a general enquiry and has no prior personal relationship with Daniel. Under Vivian's direction, Rowan plants the number and star in a manipulated **derived** contact export, before Daniel's kidnapping.
2. Sophie finds that labelled export in Daniel's previously shared digital family folder. She mistakes the entry for a possible Daniel contact and contacts the player. The family folder is a separate source from the paper folder and the encrypted backups.
3. Sophie asks Daniel's coworker Eli about the number and tells him she contacted its owner. Eli compares records he can legitimately access and then contacts that number as Unknown. He cannot see Sophie's private conversations, group membership, player choices or location. He asks the player what they know and does not assume their answer.
4. Before his kidnapping Daniel notices the discrepancy and personally creates the physical handwritten warning card. Sophie finds the original separately in Daniel's paper folder only at the end of Chapter 1. Later Daniel's handwritten note and an older photograph of the folder support a documented custody and production chain; handwriting resemblance alone is insufficient.
5. Rachel verifies the location through corroborated contractor vehicle records, harbor storage access logs, witness evidence and independent location confirmation using appropriate investigative procedures. She determines the official rescue; the player does not conduct it.

**Proposed, pending final approval:** Lucas is the custodian of a second encrypted backup Daniel entrusted to him as a safeguard. He does not know its full contents. His earlier “another copy” refers to that sealed backup, without identifying its contents or conclusively identifying it to the player in Chapter 1. The story must retain an explicit, Lucas-authorised Maya relay if the player declines direct Lucas contact; relay permission is specific and not group permission.
