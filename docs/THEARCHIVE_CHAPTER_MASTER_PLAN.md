## Shared interaction and branch contract

- Incoming notification arrival is separate from opening, reading, acknowledging and completing. Player messages require persisted selection and visible **Send**, including nested replies.
- Installation, recovery start, evidence inspection, finding Save, disclosure Review and final Send remain distinct actions. No accepted package silently installs TRACE.
- Required actions are visibly marked using the existing small green guidance dots, with a static reduced-motion form; optional actions retain optional labels. A dot never performs an action.
- Trust/tension affect the response and route to an equivalent source; they cannot permanently remove the main mystery. A declined interaction remains resumable. Safe official evidence access substitutes for optional NPC cooperation where needed.
- An optional recipient-specific Send informs only its actual recipients. A private journal, group proposal, old membership or chapter completion flag is not a knowledge receipt.
- Evidence is acquired through authored sources. TRACE may organise and compare; it cannot invent missing words, verify a voice from resemblance, infer a person from a device event, or fill a camera gap.

## Scene-level knowledge audit rule

Each row above is the maximum new knowledge for that scene, not an automatic grant to its cast. Future authoring must attach a ledger to every fact: source ID, actual inspected fragment, time available, player observation, permitted recipients and delivered recipient receipts. Writer truth never substitutes for one of those records.

Before accepting a scene, compare every NPC line against that ledger. A fact absent on a branch needs a conditional line, an explicit new disclosure, or omission. Optional dialogue can enrich motive without becoming hidden mandatory evidence. The scene matrix and state spec define completion independently of mere reading, elapsed time or relationship score.

## Handoffs and failure recovery

Every scene ends with an authored beat and a visible manual next action. Notifications may arrive at a saved deadline, but never open themselves. Required Home return in C1S10 is an explicit event, distinct from loading a save on Home. An unanswered call is pending/missed and resumable; it does not complete the chapter. Installation refusal preserves the package route without fabricated consent. Interrupted recovery resumes saved work; viewing a later fragment never overwrites the earlier delivered text or its source version.

Before each future chapter release, verify ten numbered definitions, all predecessor and manual entry guards, optional-route convergence, actual source acquisition, no premature identity/culprit/fate leak, once-only chapter unlock and historical-save continuation. The old 18-scene registry, old Bulletin path, delivered Scenes 4–6 and historical Chapter 2 access/reunion remain separate compatibility obligations. New scene numbers alone never retag or complete those saves.
