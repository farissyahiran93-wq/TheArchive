## Timeline and consistency acceptance

The [continuity recommendation](THEARCHIVE_STARRED_NUMBER_CONTINUITY.md) proposes 31 October–8 November 2026 for the nine playable days and explicit pre-play dates. All dates remain subject to approval. Before approving each scene:

1. Check all displayed times against source timezone/clock uncertainty and story day. A filesystem time is not a witnessed human action.
2. Check the actual speaker's receipt history and group membership at the delivery moment. No knowledge is retroactively granted on joining.
3. Check physical custody of every card/copy against the relevant scene. Two objects sharing a subject do not become one object.
4. Check each mandatory clue has an authorized obtainable source even on privacy/refusal routes. Pausing a required consent gate is acceptable; pretending it happened is not.
5. Check each conclusion against evidence available at that point. No later proof rewrites an earlier source-unverified label or historical message.
6. Check the final explanation pays off the number origin, warning, two backups, June's death, Rowan's choices, Eli's omissions, Vivian's responsibility and Daniel's rescue.

**Planning completion criterion:** all questions have a canon anchor, candidate solution and explicit decision boundary. **Implementation readiness criterion:** the critical items affecting the next scene are approved with sources and a knowledge ledger. This register meets the first criterion; it does not claim that open plot decisions are resolved canon or that the 50 scenes are implemented.
