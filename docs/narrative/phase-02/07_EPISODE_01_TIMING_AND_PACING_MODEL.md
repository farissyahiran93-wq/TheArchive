# Episode 01 active-play pacing — Phase 02C route estimate

**16 October 2026; unmeasured design hypothesis.** The earlier 185-minute allocation was a target budget, not evidence of duration. No playable Episode 01, timed human paper read or Android test exists. These estimates use the selected [SOCIAL and QUIET paper routes](11_PHASE_02C_PAPER_PLAY_TRACES.md), not the total 15,095 Act words: parallel options and optional dialogue are not read by one player. Fictional story time and waiting for an inbox response do not count as active play.

## Counting method and assumptions

The route tables identify **18 explicit outgoing Sends** on SOCIAL and **3** on QUIET; these are observed in the selected *script trace*, not user telemetry. Each Act's likely visible words below is an editorial estimate of the selected on-screen dialogue, UI, source passages and feedback read once, including the B01 and E012 portions that route opens. It is **not** a precise generated transcript count: conditional text, reading of source drawers and wrong-answer hints depend on reader behavior. The six Act source files contain many mutually exclusive lines, so their full word count cannot be used as a one-run reading total. Assume 150–210 visible English words per minute, plus the stated ranges for deliberate comparison, navigation, citation, choices and plausible retries. No typing speed, idle wait, automatic animation, reload, mandatory reread or artificial timer is counted. S28's range assumes an eligible fictional export is issued; a refusal holds the episode rather than increasing a successful run's measured duration.

These are deliberately **low-confidence planning ranges**, not timed test results. The QUIET route reads fewer messages but uses more error/recovery actions in this paper trace; a skilled quiet player could finish sooner. Optional source exploration, rereading or a different branch may move either route outside these ranges. The numbers expose a probable gap to 185 minutes. The recommended next measured target is to test the manuscript as an approximately **75–120-minute candidate** rather than promising three hours. The original 185-minute aspiration can be reconsidered after actual blind reads and a playable prototype. Do not pad the game with repeated chat, waits or extra puzzles merely to meet it.

## Measurement required during integration

At a timed blind paper read and later a playable build, capture active minutes by Act, selected branch, Send count, source-card dwell, puzzle attempts/hints, optional panels, and exit comprehension. Exclude idle time and backgrounded app time. At S12 ask what the editor-directory check proves; at S21 ask which dates remain only reported; at S29 ask what K02 establishes; at S30 ask whether an operator is identified. Use median/range across actual players before making a duration claim. Script route estimates and static mock tests are **not** Gate B timing evidence.
