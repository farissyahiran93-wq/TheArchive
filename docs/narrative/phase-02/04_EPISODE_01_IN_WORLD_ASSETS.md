## P06 — Public correction guide

**In-game route:** `records.greyhaven.local/help/corrections` (fictional, local-only) · **Publisher:** Civic Portal Validation Desk · **Audience:** public · **Status:** general process guide, not case-specific evidence. **Media:** text page and accessible list; no art required for its meaning. **Alt text:** “Public guide explains that a verified version is retained when a correction creates a newer public version.”

> **How a public record changes**
>
> The public page shows the current view of a record. A version marked verified is retained as a historical version when a later correction is accepted. A correction creates a new version; it does not erase the older version from the independent archived-version service.
>
> A public revision notice may show the covered record, the approval time and a short reason. The short reason is supplied for public navigation. It is not a finding that the old record was false or that the new description is historically complete.
>
> The Civic Portal Validation Desk handles the current page and its public notices. Greyhaven Civic Records Office keeps an archived-version view under a separate release process. An eligible requester can ask for a redacted lineage; private resident fields remain protected.
>
> An approval, a publication and the event described in a record are different moments. A reader should compare their labels before drawing a conclusion.

This guide explains a process only. It cannot authenticate E001, establish E012, identify an approving person or prove a 2019 motive.
