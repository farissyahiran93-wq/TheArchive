# Preservation denial: two coherent routes and their costs

**Status: PROPOSED branch design under conditional D06.** SB2 fixes the 15 Oct 2026 disposal risk, not the eventual disposition of the original. The owner's 01E brief recommends two possible outcomes; it does not approve a new power or revised ending. This design separates *investigation completion* from *preservation success*. It never reconstructs a destroyed original or grants the player authority.

## Alternative A — early independent risk direction after CRCU pause refusal

**When:** CRCU returns a signed `DEN-13-02` on **13 Oct**, or a deficiency cannot be repaired before its proposed 14:00 E07 close. The negative decision is not a pause. `R11`, the 26 Sep notice and already issued E012/E013/E014 are available only if their own custodian checks passed.

1. **13 Oct E07-S13–S19:** player inspects denial grounds and prepares a *record-risk* packet; eligible counsel/requester, never the player as an official, signs. E009 stays unavailable. E07-S20 welfare signal remains unverified. An informational 13 Oct lodging may be logged, but it does not suspend disposal.
2. **14 Oct after E08, proposed 09:05:** counsel submits `PRES-20261014-RISK-01` to GIRRO. Proposed `GH-IR-02` would permit GIRRO to preserve a *specifically identified record at imminent risk* before any merits inspection. Grounds are the authenticated folder locator, deadline and independent discrepancy, **not** a claim that Vivian's order has already been proved. GIRRO's risk-only jurisdiction is the unresolved D06 authority question.
3. **14 Oct, proposed by 12:00:** if a rostered GIRRO duty officer issues `DIR-14-RISK-01`, CRCU verifies its signature through a separate callback and signs `ACK-14-RISK-01`. Only then is there an effective E020/R15. A direction without ACK remains `ISSUED_UNACKNOWLEDGED`, not safe.
4. **After ACK:** CRCU may supervise inspection and issue a certified E009 derivative with a new copy receipt, subject to the approved access clause. The original remains in custody. E09's historical attribution work runs after that release; E10-S12 verifies the existing E020 rather than issuing a second fictional hold.

**Consequence:** loss of the easy 13 Oct inspection, extra challenge time and a narrower early packet; counsel must defend risk grounds without the carbon order. A denial or delayed ACK at this stage falls through to Alternative B. No automatic success, hidden timer or out-of-order knowledge grant is allowed.

## Alternative B — independent-evidence continuation after direction failure

**When:** GIRRO refuses after a named deficiency/review, does not issue by the safe 14 Oct gate, or CRCU does not acknowledge service. This branch also covers a pause initially granted but expiring before a valid direction. No new official preservation power is invented. A request, appeal, public statement or counsel deposit cannot itself stop CRCU's scheduled job.

1. **14 Oct E09-S19/S20 and E10-S01–S12:** counsel can repair a missing field once using an already held source; a substantive refusal is recorded as `DEN-14-07`. If `DIR-14-07` exists but no CRCU acknowledgment, record `UNACK-14-07` with attempted service time and reviewer. In both cases `preservationEffective=false`; **no E020, R15 or K14**.
2. **14 Oct E10-S14/S15:** player assembles a **separately sourced** redacted packet from items already lawfully held: E012, E013, E014, E018, E015/E019 and optionally consented E006. An eligible person submits it through the already introduced oversight intake or to counsel for documented custody using a proposed `IND-SUB-14-01`. The receiving office merely acknowledges receipt for later review; it does **not** order Crestline to preserve anything. A public-pressure choice publishes only a separately checked, consent-safe subset and does not cure missing proof.
3. **15 Oct E10-S19:** request the custodian's disposal status and check its signed job log. If destruction is independently confirmed, mark `ORIGINAL_LOST`; E009 is permanently unavailable on this route unless a **pre-existing, separately authenticated copy** had already been lawfully issued before loss. The design does not invent such a copy to rescue the player. If status is pending or unknown, say so; do not narrate a destruction or a victory.
4. **E10-S16/S20:** the board can establish a defensible **version-tampering and route/harm case** at the level supported by the independent records. E015/E019 can identify Vivian's false-correction approval and Daniel's publication if those artifacts were released and verified. Without E009 or equivalent independently authenticated *pre-existing* order evidence, the player's claim that Vivian personally issued the **21:48 diversion** stays `UNRESOLVED_FOR_PUBLIC_ATTRIBUTION`; the fixed author truth is not rewritten. The epilogue says an independent review/record-correction request has been lodged, not granted, and June's family has a sourced challenge but no guaranteed formal vindication or conviction.

**Consequence:** original physical order and warning may be lost, evidentiary weight and specificity decrease, the official correction may be slower or contested, and the characters must acknowledge that procedure failed. Ana can remain unnamed; Daniel still answers for v2 publication; Sophie need not forgive him. The player may finish a **qualified Season 1 investigation** without a fake preservation success. Owner ratification is necessary because P01's original E10 mandatory E020/full attribution promise would otherwise be changed.

## Comparison and choice rules

| Test | A: early GIRRO direction | B: independent-evidence continuation |

| Trigger | CRCU pause denied/delayed; GIRRO still willing/able to decide before disposal. | Formal direction denied, late or unacknowledged; original at risk or confirmed lost. |

| E009/E020 | E009 may follow effective E020; never precedes lawful access. | E020 absent. E009 absent if never previously copied; no magic recovery. |
| Case claim | Full attribution may become supportable after E009+E015+E019 and safety notice cross-check. | False correction/route/harm can be supported; individual diversion order remains unproved without independent direct order. |
| Failure handling | If GIRRO refuses or CRCU does not ACK, branch B. | If key independent releases fail, only lower-confidence case and truthful unresolved ending; do not promise case victory. |
| Callback | E07 denial remembered in E09/E10; E10-S19 checks protected original. | E10-S19 confirms loss or uncertainty; E10-S20 reflects cost, official review pending and June family impact. |

**Unresolved D06 decision:** Does Greyhaven's fictional charter grant GIRRO a risk-only direction that CRCU must acknowledge, and does the owner approve a qualified Season 1 ending if effective preservation fails? If either answer is no, Alternative A or B must be redesigned. The current packet makes both consequences explicit but does not declare the denial route production-ready.
