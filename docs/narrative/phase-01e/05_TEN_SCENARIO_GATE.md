# T01–T10: complete documentary simulations

**Review standard:** a `PASS` requires an end-to-end route backed by approved source authority, explicit scene triggers, recipient-scoped receipts and a reachable outcome. An internally coherent **proposed** policy is insufficient. `PARTIAL` means a trace exists but policy, scene adoption or technical verification is missing. `BLOCKED` means the claimed full result has no authorized route under a supported branch. These are paper simulations; no game, Android, timing or save test was executed. Source IDs and candidate scene rules are in [01](01_SOURCE_AUDIT_AND_DECISION_CLOSURE.md), [03](03_CONDITIONAL_POLICIES_AND_RECEIPTS.md) and [04](04_SCENE_TRIGGER_REPAIR_MANIFEST.md).
