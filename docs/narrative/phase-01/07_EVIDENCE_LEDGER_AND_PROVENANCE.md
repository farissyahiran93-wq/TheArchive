# 07 — Evidence ledger, proof standards and acquisition routes

**Private spoilers.** SB2 §8 supplies E001–E011 concept IDs; the v3 ledger preserves those meanings while adding subsidiary IDs `E012–E020` for source control. Exact document UI and release process are proposals pending D03–D08. `OBSERVED` = player can inspect; `REPORTED` = a person or article says it; `INFERRED` = a defensible hypothesis; `PROVED` = the story has independent, provenance-checked support. A lead/corroborated/established badge is a *case assessment*, not a legal ruling. Each artifact requires owner/custodian, original or copy status, acquisition receipt, in-world time, redactions and limitations.


Low-trust, no-romance proof uses E012/E003, E013/E014, E008/E018, E009/E015/E019 and E020 through public/custodial channels. E017 may strengthen the human account but must not be the sole mandatory key. No conclusion turns on a photo timestamp, phone GPS pin, an enhanced audio syllable, or an unconsented identity reveal.

## Validation checklist per artifact

Every future content file should include `evidenceId`, custodian, original creation time, acquisition time, version, permission/consent scope, hash or page count where applicable, redaction note, observation text, permissible inference, prohibited inference, related IDs, fallback route and citation. [NIST's evidence preservation guidance](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ir/2022/NIST.IR.8387.pdf) informs the provenance model; this is fiction and does not claim a real legal chain has been met.
