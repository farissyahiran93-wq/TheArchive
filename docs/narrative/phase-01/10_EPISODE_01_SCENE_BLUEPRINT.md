# 10 — Episode 01 detailed scene blueprint: THE MISSING ENTRY

**Private production design, not final dialogue or implemented gameplay.** Proposed ID `E01`; date 8 Oct 2026, fictional 09:00–18:00. SB2 Prologue + Ch1 supplies the spine; P01 requires a longer episode. This blueprint preserves the civilian opening, anonymous/pseudonymous tip and Maya reply while moving the *episode-ending* credential hook after a real chain of investigations. Every procedural detail dependent on D03/D04/D18–D20 remains `P` until approval. No Vivian culpability, Ana identity, Daniel location, `7-14` meaning or final-case truth appears in player-facing E01.

## Episode contract

- **Thematic question:** What can the player honestly infer from two versions of a document?
- **Emotional thesis:** An ordinary person's careful observation earns a place in a community investigation; it does not confer instant expertise or trust.
- **Primary player goal:** compare a public preview and current record, submit a scoped tip, verify Maya's channel, voluntarily enter the Archive workspace, and establish a post-verification revision from a genuine audit export.
- **Cast:** player and Maya in live contact. Daniel appears only in a dated note/transfer context; Vivian may be seen in a neutral old public item. No direct Daniel message.
- **Six acts / active-play budget:** A1 Ordinary attention 0–22 min; A2 Tip and reply 22–43; A3 Voluntary workspace 43–70; A4 Version chain 70–125; A5 Human context and request 125–155; A6 Verification and hook 155–185. Total **185 minutes estimated**, within a 165–195 design range. Functional split: 50 reading/social, 90 puzzle/source checks, 20 media/zoom, 25 optional exploration. These are *estimates only*; no measured episode exists.
- **Hard boundary:** E01 ends only after E012 is authenticated and E003's dates are aligned. If D03 rejects self-service export, the scene needs a new approved same-day source; do not simply declare the thumbnail “verified.”

## Representative short conversations — original samples, not final script

Each line is a delivered message. A player line appears only after the player selects a draft and presses **Send**. No automatic player response is implied; alternate options are described after each sample. Typing intervals are brief and do not count toward the episode duration estimate.

### A. First Maya reply via anonymous return token (11 messages)

1. **Maya:** “Hi. I'm Maya Chen. I put the Greyhaven case brief together.”
2. **Maya:** “Your note came through the Bulletin inbox. I can reply here without seeing your name.”
3. **Player [Send]:** “How do I know this is really you?”
4. **Maya:** “Fair. The brief has an editor card. It lists this inbox's return code.”
5. **Player [Send]:** “I checked. The code matches.”
6. **Maya:** “Thank you for checking.”
7. **Maya:** “Which line changed? I don't want to guess from a thumbnail.”
8. **Player [Send]:** “June Ellis is on the older escort list. She isn't on the current one.”
9. **Maya:** “The copy Daniel sent me still had her name.”
10. **Player [Send]:** “Does that mean the current file is wrong?”
11. **Maya:** “It means we need the version record before we say why.”

Handle variant: Maya uses the chosen handle only after the player supplied it; no legal-name assumption. Options at line 3 can instead ask “Which copy do you have?”; line 11 still defends source-checking. The player may stay anonymous throughout.

### B. Archive permission and boundaries (10 messages)

1. **Maya:** “I can invite you to the Memory Project's case workspace.”
2. **Player [Send]:** “What would that give you access to?”
3. **Maya:** “Nothing on your phone. It's a read-only place for files we are allowed to share.”
4. **Maya:** “Some files are only previews. The source card says which.”
5. **Player [Send]:** “Can I look without joining a group chat?”
6. **Maya:** “Yes. Those are separate choices.”
7. **Player [Send]:** “And if I stop?”
8. **Maya:** “You can leave. The public links will still be there.”
9. **Player [Send]:** “Send the invitation. I'll read the terms first.”
10. **Maya:** “Done. Take your time.”

The final consent button is a separate player action after line 10. Declining delays workspace-specific cards but does not erase the public audit route or trigger an insult.

### C. Cautious interpretation after a source check (10 messages)

1. **Maya:** “The portal calls it a route reconciliation.”
2. **Player [Send]:** “That could be ordinary. Why remove June?”
3. **Maya:** “I don't know. Daniel asked me to keep the older preview.”
4. **Player [Send]:** “Was that before he disappeared?”
5. **Maya:** “On the third. I can show you the transfer date, not his reason.”
6. **Player [Send]:** “Let's get the audit copy.”
7. **Maya:** “Agreed. The civic index has a request form.”
8. **Player [Send]:** “If it confirms the order of versions, what then?”
9. **Maya:** “Then we ask who approved a change after verification.”
10. **Maya:** “We still won't know why.”

An accusatory choice at line 2 changes Maya's immediate defensiveness and E03 callback, not the export route or factual result. No player line is sent by a timed scene transition.

## Accessibility, anti-spoiler and QA acceptance

- All visual differences have accessible text equivalents, zoom and high contrast. No color-only clue or timed response. Chat maintains the reader's scroll position; new lines offer an indicator rather than dragging them to bottom.
- Every player bubble has a committed Send receipt. Test tapping a choice, leaving and returning, and refreshing: no player bubble should appear before Send.
- E001 crop and E002 portal copy are leads until E012 validates provenance. Do not use “PROVED” on the first difference.
- Maya never knows the player's real identity from an anonymous token, never auto-adds them to a group, and does not possess all official records by fiat.
- The E01 ending shows role continuity without Vivian, Ana, Daniel's location, Box 7/Folder 14 or Session 02 expansion. Source cards may seed labels, not answers.
- Episode completion must persist and fire once, with no duplicated Maya replies on resume. No early E02 events.
- A first-time participant should explain in their own words who Daniel is, why Maya replied, what changed, what is still unproved and why they chose to keep investigating. Measure that in Phase 02 playtests.
