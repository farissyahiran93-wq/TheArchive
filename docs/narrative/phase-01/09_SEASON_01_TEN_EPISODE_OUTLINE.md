# 09 — Season 01: ten-episode master outline

**Private spoilers; proposed v3.** Fixed truth is SB2 §§2–5. Titles and ten-episode format are P01. Exact scene IDs, trigger times and procedural repairs are proposed and need owner approval. A scene is a bounded player action or conversation beat, **not** a finished dialogue script. Active-play budgets are estimates and exclude forced real-world waits. `S` IDs are stable *design* IDs only; do not reuse legacy runtime chapter IDs without migration. Read [04](04_MASTER_TIMELINE_2019_2026.md), [06](06_KNOWLEDGE_AND_DISCLOSURE_MATRIX.md) and [07](07_EVIDENCE_LEDGER_AND_PROVENANCE.md) alongside this outline.

In the E02–E10 scene tables, the **player is the participant in every scene**; the named speaker, correspondent or record custodian is the other participant when present. A row without another named person is a solo inspection. The first clause is the player trigger; the app and material after the semicolon identify the surface, incoming evidence and narrative purpose; the arrow names the scene's exit condition. Optional scenes have a skip edge to the next required scene. The investigation contracts below name the verification and recovery that cannot fit in every scene row.

## Season-wide progression

The first act asks whether the two files differ; the middle asks what happened to people on Bus 4 and who may safely tell that story; the last asks who ordered, who published, what must be preserved, and how to correct the record without another injury. Vivian appears in early public materials, but evidence against her accumulates only through independent records. Daniel's return adds accountability, not a solution key. Low-trust and no-romance play can complete every mandatory evidence route.
