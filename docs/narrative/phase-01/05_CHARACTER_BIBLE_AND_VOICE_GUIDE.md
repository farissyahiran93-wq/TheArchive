# 05 — Character bible and voice guide

**Private spoilers.** Ages, roles and core arcs are from SB2 §4; word-level examples below are **new test snippets**, not finished dialogue. All ages are as stated for 2026 unless noted. No precise birthdays are invented. Character dialogue is natural contemporary English; technical terms are explained in context. The player is an adult civilian with optional pronouns (SB2 §1; P01).

## Conversation design rules for everyone

- Each speaker has a reason to be in the conversation, a bounded knowledge ledger, and an availability window. Do not make every contact reply instantly or always discuss the case.
- Reply delays suggest reading or checking a source (typically 1–3 seconds of UI typing for short text); a genuine multi-hour fictional absence is marked by an authored scene transition with other playable tasks, not a real-world timer.
- Calls require scheduling, consent/availability and captions or a transcript. A missed call can be replayed as a voicemail or summarized without losing mandatory proof.
- Emotional shifts follow a prior action and a receipt. A single flattering line cannot create deep trust; a low-trust character may cooperate through documented duty instead of intimacy.
- Romance eligibility, if approved, is adult, reciprocal, optional and separate from access to facts. Sophie is grieving and receives no early flirt prompts; her brother's safety is never a romance currency (decision D13).

## Voice QA pass

For every authored conversation, record: scene ID; speaker's goal; facts known *before* line; normal/max reply length; interruptions; emotional trigger; whether an emoji would be out of character; what remains unsaid; and one non-case detail. A reviewer should be able to swap character names and detect a voice mismatch. The three Episode 01 sample conversations in [10](10_EPISODE_01_SCENE_BLUEPRINT.md) demonstrate mechanics, not final prose.
