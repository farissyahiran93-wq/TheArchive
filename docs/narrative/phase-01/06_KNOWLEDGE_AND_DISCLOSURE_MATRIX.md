## Low-trust / no-romance path

Player sends an anonymous tip (R01), declines social invitations, and shares only necessary record details. Maya can point to public/custodial export rather than grant private trust. Lucas withholds his personal photo; dispatch custodian provides route sheet. Nora keeps Ana's name protected; a consented anonymized transcript plus independent records establishes the stop. Eli receives only a redacted audio/file and explains limits. Counsel confirms Daniel's welfare without location. Records custodian grants hold on documented grounds. The player can choose **Legal First** and submit a redacted, cited evidence packet. No romance meter or personal confession gates K02, K05, K06, K10, K13 or K14.

## QA negative assertions

- No player-authored receipt before explicit Send; choosing a response only drafts it.
- `R08` does not grant K07. `R12` grants welfare, not Daniel's address. `R14` does not automatically make the case public.
- A photo, audio clip, map pin or group invitation does not grant a fact by itself; a named recipient must access/receive and understand the content.
- A character with low trust may refuse a *personal* share, but the authored alternate route must preserve mandatory case access.
