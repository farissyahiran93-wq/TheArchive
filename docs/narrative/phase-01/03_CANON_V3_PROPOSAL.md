## Proposed operational model (`P`)

1. **Three record layers:** Crestline issues correction requests; a separate Greyhaven civic portal publishes current v2; an audit custodian retains time-stamped v1 and the approval trail. Vivian can authorize/pressure the correction workflow, not rewrite the independent audit or paper archive. Approval needed: D04.
2. **Civic-facing redaction:** The 7 Oct Bulletin brief shows June's escort entry in a cropped or redacted v1 preview and links a current redacted v2. Ana/Rowan identities remain private. A formal export later authenticates v1. Approval: D03/D11.
3. **Tip return token:** The Bulletin form creates a local fictional inbox thread. Anonymous players retain a return code and may reply; pseudonymous players expose only their chosen handle. Maya sees tip content and token, not device identity, legal name or GPS. She verifies her identity as the Memory Project case-brief editor. Approval: D18.
4. **Voluntary workspace:** Maya offers a limited, read-only invitation to THE ARCHIVE, a Memory Project evidence workspace. Consent is explicit. Documents are labeled by owner and release scope; being inside the workspace does not make them authentic. Approval: D19.
5. **Preservation ladder:** Daniel's 2 Oct report was an intake lacking a formal hold. A custodian grants a documented short disposal pause and supervised inspection on 13 Oct; after corroboration, counsel/custodian receives a complete request on 14 Oct and acknowledges a formal hold before 15 Oct. The two receipts are distinct. Approval: D05/D06.
6. **Safety/consent:** Ana can permit a redacted statement, a private identity disclosure to a named recipient, or no contact. The core case can use lawful records and anonymous content without compelling her to appear publicly. Daniel's welfare is checked through counsel; his concealment remains ethically costly. Approval: D07/D09/D10.
7. **Active credential hook:** Episode 01 shows that a *correction role/queue* still accepts work in 2026, not that Vivian personally logged in today. A live role plus missing review record motivates investigation but does not prove current tampering. Approval: D20.
