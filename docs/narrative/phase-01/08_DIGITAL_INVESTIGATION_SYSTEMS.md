# 08 — Original fictional phone and investigation systems

**Private spoilers.** Design-only. The device is a fictional Greyhaven phone environment with a **Bulletin** community service, a distinct **civic records portal**, and a voluntarily opened **THE ARCHIVE** Memory Project workspace. The latter is a source workspace; an optional **Case Board** records the player's reasoning. None controls real GPS, accounts, contacts, CCTV or email. Phase 00 provides only static/fixture app shells; implementation dependencies are in [13](13_PHASE_02_ENTRY_GATE.md).

## Realism and privacy constraints

- A GPS fix estimates a receiver's position and can degrade near buildings ([GPS.gov](https://www.gps.gov/gps-accuracy-0)); it cannot establish that Daniel carried a device. The story must display device ID, fix type, accuracy and last-known time.
- CCTV retention is purpose-specific and should not be presumed to span seven years ([ICO guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/cctv-and-video-surveillance/guidance-on-video-surveillance-including-cctv/how-can-we-comply-with-the-data-protection-principles-when-using-surveillance-systems/)). Use 2019 *news* video or an explicitly preserved evidentiary copy, not a magically accessible municipal camera feed.
- Evidence transfer logs record who handled a file and when; a hash guards integrity but does not make a false statement true ([NIST IR 8387](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ir/2022/NIST.IR.8387.pdf)). Greyhaven's custody process must be internally consistent, not copied as real-world law.
- A generator in an enclosed area plausibly creates fatal CO danger ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/carbon-monoxide/about/)); no puzzle asks the player to diagnose June from a waveform instead of documented records.
