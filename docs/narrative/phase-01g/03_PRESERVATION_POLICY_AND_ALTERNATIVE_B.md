## Branch and claim contract

1. **Pause granted + lawful supervised copy:** E009 may be issued. E09 separately checks E015/E019 and warning/role chain. GIRRO still needs a valid direction and CRCU ACK for E020/R15/K14. Pause expiry alone cannot turn a pending formal request into success.
2. **CRCU refuses pause:** E009 is unavailable. Eligible counsel can file the *risk-only* GIRRO packet using R11, notice and independent discrepancy. If GIRRO DIR is validly served and ACKed before disposal, E020 is effective; CRCU can then authorize supervised copying under the new scope. If no copy is issued, E009 remains absent even though the original was preserved.
3. **GIRRO refuses, service fails or CRCU does not ACK:** no E020/R15/K14. Requester may seek named review; player may submit a protected, already lawfully held independent-evidence packet `IND-SUB-14-01`. That receipt proves **lodging for later review only**, not an order. Public pressure cannot acquire power or cure a missing source.
4. **Confirmed loss:** require a signed, independently inspectable custodian job/status log. If no lawful E009 derivative predated loss, the carbon order and warning are gone for this route. Independent E003/E012, E013/E014, E018, consented E006 and checked E015/E019 can support version tampering, route/harm and false-correction approval at their own limits. They cannot alone publicly attribute Vivian's **individual 21:48 diversion authorization** or recreate the warning. Story Bible truth that Vivian ordered it remains fixed.
5. **Unknown/pending status:** state uncertainty; preserve inquiry and repair route. Neither saved-original victory nor destruction ending is earned. If any independent release failed, downgrade that claim too.
