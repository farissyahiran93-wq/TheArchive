## Causal chain

Vivian wants inquiries diverted from June's original safety findings. She directs Rowan to add an apparently ordinary outsider to a family-facing contact export, so a worried relative may spend time pursuing a misleading lead. Rowan takes a fictional callback number from a restricted project consultation register, selecting an uninvolved person who submitted an ordinary public enquiry; he has no reason to suppose that person knows Daniel. The register is an in-world artifact, not a real contacts lookup. He inserts that number and the star into a **derived export**, not Daniel's original phone contacts, and arranges for the altered copy to be available in Daniel's shared effects folder.

Daniel compares the export against his earlier encrypted snapshot, finds the unexpected insertion and suspects a trap. He does not know the player or identify the editor. He writes the number and star on a separate card and writes “DO NOT CONTACT THE STARRED NUMBER.” on its reverse. The warning is about an unknown planted entry, not a claim the player is dangerous. He leaves the card in his private paper folder for his own review. He intends to explain it after retrieving the original records; interception prevents him doing so. This delay is his mistake, not foreknowledge of Sophie's actions.

After Daniel's disappearance Maya passes Sophie a sealed envelope containing his paper folder; Sophie sets it aside while dealing with the immediate emergency. Separately, Sophie receives the accessible shared-folder export through Daniel's previously established family access. It is labelled as his contacts, so she calls the starred number hoping it belongs to a person who can help. She has not yet read the card. At the end of Day 1, sorting the paper folder, she finds it and calls the player; the final line is “But… I already did.” Maya did not read the card merely by carrying the envelope. The GH-DSC-03-A reference card is a different object.

Eli had received Daniel's encrypted archive at Daniel's request. After Daniel is missing, Eli compares the retained baseline with the family-facing copy using work access already available to him. He learns the inserted number from that comparison. On Day 1 Sophie sends him a narrowly scoped message asking whether he recognises the unusual contact entry; that actual delivered message tells him she has already contacted the number and invited its owner to help. It does not give him her conversation, the player's responses, private group history or location. Eli independently contacts the number as Unknown, asks what the player knows and offers the archive. He does not know whether the player accepted the invitation until told. His concern for an exposed outsider and fear for Daniel motivate the contact; fear of retaliation and his unreported earlier access failure explain, but do not excuse, his anonymity and reluctance to go directly to Rachel. The player can question him, defer and resume.
