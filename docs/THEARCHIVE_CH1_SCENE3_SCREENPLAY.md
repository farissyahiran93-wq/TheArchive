# Chapter 1 Scene 3 — Just Between Us

The existing Scene 2 handoff delivers “Hey. Are you okay after all of that?” Opening Sophie manually starts this scene. Each player choice requires Send.

**Sophie:** I'm sorry for how that went in the group.

**Sophie:** I wanted to check on you before I put my phone down.

## Decision 01

**Player A:** I'm okay. Are you?

**Sophie:** I've been better. Thank you for asking.

**Player B:** I'm all right. That got tense quickly.

**Sophie:** It did. I didn't mean to put you in the middle of it.

**Player C:** I need a little time to process it.

**Sophie:** Of course. We can take this slowly.

**Then:**

**Sophie:** I'm sorry about the group.

**Sophie:** Maya and Lucas are worried about Daniel too. They just show it differently.

**Sophie:** I should have talked to them before I invited you.

## Decision 02

**Player A:** You were trying to help your brother.

**Sophie:** I was. That doesn't mean I handled it perfectly.

**Player B:** Lucas seemed worried about privacy.

**Sophie:** He was. He's protective of Daniel, and I understand why.

**Player C:** I'd rather not be caught between you.

**Sophie:** That's fair. I won't ask you to take sides.

**Then:**

**Sophie:** Daniel's my older brother. Somehow he still manages to make me feel like the younger one, even when he's the one who needs help.

## Decision 03

**Player A:** What was he like as an older brother?

**Sophie:** Protective, mostly. Annoying too, when we were kids.

**Player B:** Did you two see each other often?

**Sophie:** As often as we could manage. Sometimes it was just a quick meal.

**Player C:** You sound close.

**Sophie:** We are. We don't always agree, but he's my brother.

**Then:**

**Sophie:** When I was younger, I once told him I was too old to need a lift home.

**Sophie:** He said fine, and drove behind the bus anyway.

**Sophie:** I was furious when I spotted him. He tried to pretend he just happened to be going the same way.

**Sophie:** I didn't believe him for a second.

## Decision 04

**Player A:** That sounds like him looking out for you.

**Sophie:** It was. I'd never have admitted that to him at the time.

**Player B:** Did he ever admit he followed the bus?

**Sophie:** Years later. He still insisted it was a coincidence, then started laughing.

**Player C:** I can see why that annoyed you.

**Sophie:** Right? He could be thoughtful and impossible in exactly the same minute.

**Then:**

**Sophie:** He'd remember the little things too. If I mentioned I had a difficult day coming up, he'd ask about it afterwards.

**Sophie:** Lately I've been reaching for my phone to tell him things, and then remembering why he hasn't answered.

## Decision 05

**Player A:** How is Maya coping?

**Sophie:** She's trying to keep going. I think the not knowing is exhausting her.

**Player B:** Were Maya and Daniel doing all right?

**Sophie:** They're engaged. Like anyone, they could disagree, but I can't tell you what was happening privately between them.

**Player C:** You don't have to speak for Maya.

**Sophie:** Thank you. I can only tell you what she's chosen to tell me.

**Then:**

**Sophie:** Maya's his fiancée. She's been there for him through a lot, and she cares about him deeply.

**Sophie:** They had ordinary disagreements. I don't want to turn that into an explanation for him being missing.

## Decision 06

**Player A:** And Lucas?

**Sophie:** He's Daniel's best friend. They've known each other a long time.

**Player B:** Did Daniel usually confide in Lucas?

**Sophie:** Sometimes. There are things Daniel told Lucas that he didn't tell me, and that's okay.

**Player C:** I can understand Lucas wanting to protect him.

**Sophie:** So can I. I just wish we could have talked without hurting each other.

**Then:**

**Sophie:** Lucas has always cared about giving Daniel space to decide what he wants to share.

**Sophie:** His leaving the chat doesn't mean he's stopped looking for him.

## Decision 07

**Player A:** Did you notice anything different before Daniel disappeared?

**Sophie:** He seemed distracted the last few times we spoke.

**Player B:** Was there something that made you worry?

**Sophie:** He'd go quiet in the middle of a conversation, like his thoughts had gone somewhere else.

**Player C:** Only if you're comfortable: how had he seemed lately?

**Sophie:** I can talk about what I noticed. I just don't know what it meant.

**Then:**

**Sophie:** I asked once if work was bothering him. He said he was tired and changed the subject.

**Sophie:** Maybe he was only tired. I can't tell you what he was thinking.

## Decision 08

**Player A:** What was your last ordinary conversation with him?

**Sophie:** We talked about dinner.

**Player B:** Do you remember what you talked about last?

**Sophie:** Yes. It was something completely ordinary: dinner.

**Player C:** It's okay if you don't want to revisit it.

**Sophie:** I do want to remember it. We were talking about dinner.

**Then:**

**Sophie:** He'd promised to bring dessert next time we saw each other.

**Sophie:** I told him that didn't count if he bought the same biscuits he always does.

**Sophie:** He said the biscuits were a family tradition. They aren't. He just likes them.

**Sophie — photo, for newly started Scene 3 conversations:** These are the biscuits I was talking about. He always bought the same ones. The optional-to-view kitchen snapshot is a personal memory, not recovered evidence.

**Sophie:** I keep thinking about how normal it sounded. I didn't know it would be the last time we'd speak before he went missing.

## Decision 09

**Player A:** You couldn't have known that.

**Sophie:** I know. I have to keep reminding myself of that.

**Player B:** Thank you for telling me about him.

**Sophie:** Thank you for listening. I didn't want him to become just a name on a screen.

**Player C:** Would it help if I gave you some space?

**Sophie:** I think I need that. Thank you for asking.

**Then:**

**Sophie:** I'm getting tired, and I should try to rest for a while.

**Sophie:** You don't have to do anything else for me tonight.

## Decision 10

**Player A:** Rest, Sophie. I'll be here when you're ready.

**Sophie:** Thank you. That means a lot.

**Player B:** Take the time you need. We can talk later.

**Sophie:** I will. Thank you for listening.

**Player C:** I'll give you space. Goodbye for now.

**Sophie:** Thank you for understanding.

**Then:**

**Sophie:** Goodbye.

Sophie goes offline. Scene 4 is unlocked without introducing Unknown or TRACE.
