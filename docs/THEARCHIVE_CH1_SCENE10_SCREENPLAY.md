# Chapter 1 Scene 10 — The Inventory Sheet

The public inventory search must be submitted, reviewed, compared and saved privately before the player returns Home and answers Sophie. The matching reference does not authenticate the card or reveal the underlying record.

## Sophie’s call

**Sophie 01:** Hi. I'm sorry to call again. I almost sent a message, but... I didn't know how to write this.

- **Player A:** It's okay. I'm here, Sophie.  
  **Sophie:** Thank you. Give me a second.
- **Player B:** What happened?  
  **Sophie:** I found something in Daniel's papers.
- **Player C:** Are you somewhere you feel safe talking?  
  **Sophie:** I'm at home. The door's locked. I just need to tell you what I found.

**Sophie 02:** Remember when we talked about that damaged remark beside the starred entry? I told you I'd look through his old papers again.

- **Player A:** I remember. You took another look?  
  **Sophie:** I did. I thought I'd already checked, but I missed a folder.
- **Player B:** Did you find anything related to the export?  
  **Sophie:** Maybe. I can't prove they're connected, but there's something you need to hear.
- **Player C:** That remark was unreadable. Let's keep it separate from anything new.  
  **Sophie:** You're right. What I found is a separate piece of paper.

**Sophie 03:** It was in the family paper folder. A small handwritten card, tucked between some old documents. Maya brought the folder over earlier; she didn't go through it.

- **Player A:** That must have been difficult to open.  
  **Sophie:** It was. I keep expecting his things to tell me where he is.
- **Player B:** Can you tell me exactly where you found it?  
  **Sophie:** Between two sheets in Daniel's family paper folder. I can't say who put it there.
- **Player C:** Did anyone else see the card?  
  **Sophie:** Not that I know of. Maya brought the folder, but she didn't read the papers.

**Sophie 04:** At first I thought it was another one of his reminders. The handwriting looks like Daniel's. But I haven't had anyone check it.

- **Player A:** I know his handwriting would be familiar to you.  
  **Sophie:** It is. But I don't want my wanting it to be his to make it true.
- **Player B:** Does the card have a date or signature?  
  **Sophie:** I can't see either. It's just the number, a star, and a message.
- **Player C:** It could resemble his writing without proving who wrote it.  
  **Sophie:** I know. I can tell you what it looks like, not who wrote it.

**Sophie 05:** There's a number on the card. A little star next to it. I checked it against the number I used when I first messaged you.

- **Player A:** Take your time. What did you find?  
  **Sophie:** I checked it twice. It's the same number.
- **Player B:** Do the numbers match exactly?  
  **Sophie:** Yes. Every digit I can see matches the number I used.
- **Player C:** Please don't read my number aloud.  
  **Sophie:** I won't. But the number on the card matches yours.

**Sophie 06:** I kept hoping I'd copied it wrong. So I put the two side by side. I don't think I did.

- **Player A:** You're not alone in this, Sophie.  
  **Sophie:** Thank you. I'm trying not to let my mind run ahead.
- **Player B:** Is there anything else written on the card?  
  **Sophie:** Yes. There's a warning, underneath the number.
- **Player C:** A matching number doesn't tell us why it was written there.  
  **Sophie:** I know. But there is something else written underneath it.

**Sophie 07:** There's a warning. I don't know who wrote it or when. I've been staring at it, hoping there was another way to read it.

- **Player A:** It's all right. You can read it to me.  
  **Sophie:** Okay. I'll read it exactly as it is.
- **Player B:** What does it say, exactly?  
  **Sophie:** I'll read the words on the card.
- **Player C:** Tell me only what's actually written.  
  **Sophie:** I will. I won't guess what it means.

**Sophie 08:** I need you to understand... this isn't the reference card Maya showed you. This was with Daniel's papers. It has your number, and the star.

- **Player A:** I understand. What does the warning say?  
  **Sophie:** Okay.
- **Player B:** I'm listening. Read it exactly.  
  **Sophie:** Okay.
- **Player C:** I understand they're separate. Go on.  
  **Sophie:** Okay.

**Sophie:** DO NOT CONTACT THE STARRED NUMBER.

*The separate handwritten card displays the player’s fictional number and a star. Authorship is not authenticated.*

**Sophie:** But… I already did.

*Pause. Chapter 1 completes only after the player continues; Chapter 2 unlocks without starting.*
