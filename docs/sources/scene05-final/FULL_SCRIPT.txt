======================================================================

Scene 04 is the direct predecessor. Its last required beat is Sophie's
Goodnight/offline moment, after the player has inspected the six-paragraph
GB-ARTICLE-001 and the limited ARC-IDX-GB-041 metadata.

The following are fixed:
* GB-ARTICLE-001 is the Nora Vance bylined GREYHAVEN BULLETIN article,
  publication date 7 October 2026. Its displayed label is precisely
  "Updated: Yesterday", an unverified relative label.
* GB-ARTICLE-001 and ARC-IDX-GB-041 are TWO DIFFERENT RECORDS.
* ARC-IDX-GB-041: source UNKNOWN; author/capture time NOT ESTABLISHED;
  verified original NO; content preview UNAVAILABLE. No recovered text,
  confirmed editor or version diff is implied.
* Earlier CH1-PUBLIC / Maya-editor / Bus 4 content belongs to the historical
  structural route. NEVER treat it as the older edition of GB-ARTICLE-001.
* Existing EV-PUBLIC-GB-001 and EV-INDEX-GB-041 remain verified:false.
* Sophie knows only what the player actually told her in Scene 04,
  plus the public article she herself linked. If the player did not
  disclose index details, Sophie cannot quote the index reference.
* Maya knows only messages delivered to her in prior scenes and any
  public material she independently encountered; no private player Board.
* Lucas does not automatically return to FIND DANIEL. No Eli/Nora private
  contact is forged. Unknown Sender blocked/unread branches persist.
* The player is an ordinary civilian, not a forensic expert or police officer.
* Choices are composer drafts until the player taps Send. Silence and
  exiting the app produce NO outgoing player line.
* Prior character relationship and evidence states are additive. Existing
  saves remain playable. Do not overwrite old message histories.

The story MUST NOT in Scene 05 prove that the article text was changed,
who changed it, why it was changed, that Daniel is alive/dead, or that
ARC-IDX-GB-041 is an authenticated earlier version.

Narrative question: "What would it take to prove that this story changed?"
Emotional question: "How do you help someone without selling them certainty?"
