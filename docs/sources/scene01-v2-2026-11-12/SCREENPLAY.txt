GREYHAVEN — Chapter 1, Scene 1: A Message from Sophie
Final in-game screenplay — Sophie Mercer
Version 2 · Nine decision points × three player replies · English · photo embedded in the ordinary chat · no separate photo-reply UI.
Opening (NPC messages, one bubble at a time)
Sophie: Hi. Sorry to message you out of nowhere.
Sophie: I'm Sophie.
Sophie: I know this is strange.
Sophie: Could I ask you something?  
Decision 01 — An unfamiliar message
- Player A: Sure. What's this about?
  - Sophie, if A: Thank you. I wasn't sure you'd answer.
- Player B: Who are you trying to reach?
  - Sophie, if B: You, I think. I may have the wrong number.
- Player C: Before I answer, how did you get this number?
  - Sophie, if C: That's a fair question. I found it in some paperwork. I'll explain.
Sophie: It's about my older brother.
Sophie: His name is Daniel Mercer.
Sophie: Does that name mean anything to you?  
Decision 02 — The name
- Player A: No, I don't think I know him.
  - Sophie, if A: Okay. Thank you for being honest.
- Player B: Daniel Mercer? That doesn't ring a bell.
  - Sophie, if B: I thought it might not. I'm sorry to put you on the spot.
- Player C: Who is Daniel, and why would I know him?
  - Sophie, if C: He's my brother. I found your number among some records connected to him. I don't know why it's there.
Sophie: A name might not mean much on its own.
Sophie: Would you mind looking at a photo?  

Sophie shares existing birthday photograph: /media/chat/sophie-birthday-owner.png
Sophie caption (next normal chat bubble): That's Daniel on the left. It was my birthday. Does his face look familiar?
Next: Decision 03 (the only reply choices visible in the bottom composer).
Decision 03 — The birthday photograph
- Player A: I'm sorry, Sophie. I don't recognise him.
  - Sophie, if A: Thank you for looking. I know it was a lot to ask.
- Player B: Is Daniel the one on the left? He doesn't look familiar.
  - Sophie, if B: Yes. That's Daniel. Thank you for checking.
- Player C: I don't know him, but that looks like a happy memory.
  - Sophie, if C: It was. I nearly forgot how happy he looked that night.
Sophie: He made some ridiculous joke right before that picture.
Sophie: I wish I could remember exactly what he said.
Sophie: No one close to him has heard from him in three days.  
Decision 04 — Three days of silence
- Player A: Three days? Has anyone heard from him at all?
  - Sophie, if A: Not anyone I've spoken to. I've asked the people closest to him.
- Player B: What do you mean, no one's heard from him?
  - Sophie, if B: He stopped answering calls and messages. Then nobody knew where he'd gone.
- Player C: Is that why you're contacting strangers?
  - Sophie, if C: Yes. I know how desperate it must look.
Sophie: At first I told myself he'd just lost his phone.
Sophie: Then I kept telling myself that.
Sophie: Now I don't know what to think.  
Decision 05 — Why this number?
- Player A: How did you get my number, Sophie?
  - Sophie, if A: I found it in a saved contact export among the family files we could access.
- Player B: Did Daniel give you my contact details?
  - Sophie, if B: I can't say that he did. The number was in a saved contact export, but I don't know who added it.
- Player C: Why would his disappearance lead you to me?
  - Sophie, if C: Your number was in a contact export among Daniel's family files. I thought I should at least ask.
Sophie: Most of the entries had names.
Sophie: Yours didn't.
Sophie: Just a number, with a little star beside it.  
Decision 06 — The starred entry
- Player A: A star? Do you know what it means?
  - Sophie, if A: No. There's no explanation beside it.
- Player B: That doesn't prove Daniel knew me.
  - Sophie, if B: You're right. I shouldn't treat it as proof.
- Player C: Could someone else have put my number there?
  - Sophie, if C: Maybe. I have no way to tell who added it.
Sophie: I almost didn't send you this message.
Sophie: I kept typing it, then deleting it.
Sophie: I was afraid of bothering the wrong person. But I was more afraid of missing something.  
Decision 07 — A boundary, not an obligation
- Player A: I can listen. I can't promise anything more.
  - Sophie, if A: That's more than fair. Thank you for hearing me out.
- Player B: Please don't share my number or this conversation.
  - Sophie, if B: I won't share either without asking you first.
- Player C: I'd prefer to keep my distance from all of this.
  - Sophie, if C: I understand. I won't pressure you.
Sophie: I know you don't owe me anything.
Sophie: I just wish I had one thing I could be certain of.  
Decision 08 — The people looking for Daniel
- Player A: Who else is looking for him?
  - Sophie, if A: Maya and Lucas. Maya's his fiancée, and Lucas is his best friend.
- Player B: Have you told his friends or family about this?
  - Sophie, if B: Yes. Maya is his fiancée. Lucas is his closest friend. We're trying to work out what we actually know.
- Player C: Are you trying to find him on your own?
  - Sophie, if C: No. Maya and Lucas are helping. We're just people who care about him.
Sophie: We made a private group called FIND DANIEL.
Sophie: It's mostly questions, and the small things we can confirm.
Sophie: If you'd like, I could send you an invitation. No pressure.  
Decision 09 — An invitation, not a demand
- Player A: Send the invitation. I'll take a look.
  - Sophie, if A: Okay. You'll still get to choose whether to join.
- Player B: I need some time before I decide.
  - Sophie, if B: Of course. You can decide later.
- Player C: I'd rather not join right now.
  - Sophie, if C: I understand. I won't add you.
Sophie: Whatever you decide, thank you for answering.
Sophie: I didn't know whether anyone would.  

Scene transition: Existing TA-S01-INVITATION event. Player must voluntarily join before Scene 2 can begin.
Canon and performance notes
- Sophie never states that Daniel created the contact export, that the star is genuine, or who planted the number.
- Sophie does not discover the physical warning card in Scene 1; this remains for Scene 10.
- The photo is a personal memory, not forensic evidence or proof of acquaintance.
- Every outgoing player line requires explicit Send and appears as a player bubble; Sophie replies only to the selected branch.
- Sophie replies and common follow-up lines are one bubble at a time, with natural typing and response pacing.
- Group refusal is respected; no auto-join, no implicit consent, no forced app installation.
