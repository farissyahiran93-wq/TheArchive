## ACT 1A — WHAT IS TRACE?

PLAYER:

"What record?"

UNKNOWN NUMBER:

"A reference to the Bulletin article about Daniel."

"You can view it in an app called TRACE."

PLAYER:

"What is TRACE?"

UNKNOWN NUMBER:

"It lets you look through a collection of recovered references."

"Some entries point to documents."

"Others only have a title or reference number."

"An entry can be there even when the document itself is missing."

"Sometimes a copy of the document is included."

"Sometimes only the reference is left."

PLAYER:

"So it's a backup?"

UNKNOWN NUMBER:

"Not exactly."

"A backup should contain a copy of the actual document."

"This collection doesn't always have that copy."

"An entry might list a title."

"Or a reference number."

"That doesn't mean the document itself is there."

PLAYER:

"Where did it come from?"

UNKNOWN NUMBER:

"It was already incomplete when I got it."

"It was described as a collection of recovered indexes."

"I don't know who first put it together."

"Or where each entry came from."

PLAYER:

"And I'm supposed to believe that?"

UNKNOWN NUMBER:

"You don't have to."

"Check what you can see. Don't take my explanation as proof."

PLAYER:

"Why send it to me?"

UNKNOWN NUMBER:

"It points to the article about Daniel."

"You can compare the reference with the public page yourself."

PLAYER:

"What's in the reference?"

UNKNOWN NUMBER:

"The article title and a reference number."

"There's no original article text in that entry."
