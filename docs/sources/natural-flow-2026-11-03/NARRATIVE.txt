================================================================
NO-AUTO-SEND / KNOWLEDGE / SAVE CONTRACT
================================================================
1. No player line, phone message, publisher email, or corrections inquiry is sent by opening a page, finishing an animation, backgrounding, or receiving NPC text. Selection + explicit SEND is always required for chats, and REVIEW + SEND REQUEST for publisher inquiry.
2. NPCs cannot know private metadata the player has not explicitly disclosed in chat; source completion can unlock a choice, not grant character knowledge by itself.
3. An already-read Scene 4 article satisfies article read. Scene 5's SAVE ANALYSIS TO TRACE is separate. The TRACE scan is the Scene 4→5 bridge and its saved findings are reused.
4. Source results may be explored in any order, but each is discussed via its own sent player message. The player should never face a simultaneous all-source checklist.
5. Game-save migration must not reset replies, source receipts, completed scan, optional inquiries, or knowledge/relationships.
6. Evidence conclusion: no formal correction notice publicly listed; no authenticated earlier text; body revision not established; actual editor not identified; index origin unverified; Daniel's fate unresolved.
