- Preserve Lucas's justified security concerns: he can be abrasive, but he is not a one-note villain. His decision to leave comes from a fractured group, not because he is secretly culpable.
- Maya is calm, dry and precise—until an ordinary human moment slips through. Sophie alternates between trying to smooth things over and finally snapping.
- The Unknown Sender gets ONE unsettling message only: **“You shouldn't be talking to her.”** “Her” is not disambiguated; nobody is identified. No magical proof the sender is monitoring live messages; any interpretation is speculation.
- Message REQUEST is a separate thread; sender text is PRIVATE to Player unless Player later discloses it. Do not broadcast it to the group, and do not let Sophie know about it automatically.
- No usable Archive records. No recovered-note clue. The chat can mention only Daniel, the unverified starred contact, and concerns over his silence.
- **Do not auto-send player dialogue.** Every option + optional chained player response waits for the player's own Send.
