### SCENE 02 — THE UNINVITED GUEST

**Script:** Complete interactive conversation, player choices, premium branches, sender interruption, and runtime notes  
**Language:** English dialogue and implementation notes  
**Tone:** Grounded, slow-burn social suspense; restrained, occasionally funny, and human  
**Main choices:** `OPEN-S02-C01` through `OPEN-S02-C13` (13 choice points, 7 optional ★ extended choices)  
**Additional interaction:** `OPEN-S02-UNKNOWN-01`, `OPEN-S02-UNKNOWN-02`  
**Entry:** Scene 01 finished via a deliberate `JOIN GROUP`; player deliberately opens **FIND DANIEL**  
**Exit:** Lucas has left FIND DANIEL; Sophie offers a private conversation; Scene 03 starts only when the player opens her message.

> **AUTHORIAL INTENT:** “The Uninvited Guest” refers both to the player, whom Lucas never agreed to invite, and to the unrecognised sender entering the player's private messages. The two invasions should feel different: the first is a believable argument about consent and privacy, the second an inexplicable private warning.

> **CANON BOUNDARIES:** Sophie has told the player only that a *saved export labelled as Daniel Mercer's contacts* includes the player's unnamed, starred number. This is not proof Daniel personally saved or marked it. Sophie has NOT yet revealed that she is Daniel's sister; Maya has NOT yet been identified to the player as Daniel's fiancée. Lucas's childhood friendship, Eli's job, Nora, the public article and The Archive's active investigation belong to later scenes. The sender's identity, motive, knowledge and the referent of “her” are unknown. The player is not assumed to be a detective, hacker, or a previous acquaintance of Daniel.

> **ENGINE CONVENTION:** Every `PLAYER` sentence, including second sentences within premium branches, requires its own player tap and **Send**. Never spontaneously send text on behalf of the player. `CONVERGE` is a single common node; it must play once only. Pauses, typing indicators, idle windows and UI directives are not chat bubbles.
