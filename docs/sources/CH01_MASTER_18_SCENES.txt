# PART 2 — STORY FOUNDATION

## 2.1 THE PREMISE

The Player is an ordinary civilian.

They are not a trained investigator, cybersecurity expert, police officer or secret agent.

One day, an unfamiliar phone number contacts them.

The sender introduces herself as Sophie Mercer.

Her brother, Daniel Mercer, has disappeared.

Before his disappearance, Daniel saved the Player's phone number in a contact list.

Sophie does not know why.

The Player is drawn into a mystery involving Daniel's personal relationships, historical records, contradictory public documents, damaged digital files and an unexplained recovered-device system known as THE ARCHIVE.

Not everyone remembers events the same way.

Not every document is authentic.

Not every secret is evidence of guilt.

And not every person who appears helpful can independently verify their claims.

## 2.2 MAIN MYSTERY

Why did Daniel leave the Player's number behind?

What happened to the missing record?

Why do older and newer versions of historical records differ?

Who arranged the recovered-device connection?

What happened to Daniel Mercer?

## 2.3 CHAPTER 1 NARRATIVE GOAL

By the end of Chapter 1, the Player should have:

- Established meaningful contact with Sophie.
- Experienced the initial FIND DANIEL group conflict.
- Learned the basic personal relationships between Daniel and his closest contacts.
- Discovered discrepancies in publicly accessible historical records.
- Encountered Nora Vance through a credible reporting channel.
- Received Daniel's surviving note fragment.
- Encountered Unknown Sender / an unverified recovery request.
- Authorized access to The Archive.
- Explored Daniel's recovered device records.
- Identified the 14/13 index discrepancy.
- Met Eli Ward through an appropriate introduction.
- Developed separate interactions with Maya and Lucas.
- Investigated the historical revision timeline.
- Authenticated a limited conclusion about a post-verification change.
- Revisited the group conflict with a clearer purpose.
- Discovered the unexplained continuing status of a correction-related role.

The chapter must end with meaningful progress, not a solution to the complete mystery.

**Characters:** Player; unidentified source

- Give the Player a meaningful access decision.

The Player may:

- Inspect the sender information.
- Examine the recovery notice.
- Review access scope.
- Defer the request.

### Unknown Sender Restrictions

Do not invent a detailed two-way relationship with Unknown Sender.

Do not reveal identity, motive or technical authority.

Any future direct messaging must be separately authored.

### Exit Condition
