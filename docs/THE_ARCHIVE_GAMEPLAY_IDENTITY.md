# THE ARCHIVE — gameplay identity

## Function

THE ARCHIVE is the player's workspace for inspecting incomplete reconstructed records and comparing what different sources say. It presents surviving digital fragments, their versions and their limits. Its central interaction is deciding what a record supports, what is still missing, and what the player is willing to disclose to another person.

The missing-person story supplies emotional stakes. Records supply material to investigate. Player decisions connect those two layers through explicit questions and scoped sharing. A technically recovered item can still be misleading, incomplete or unauthenticated.

## Player loop

1. Receive a grounded lead through an authored message or public source.
2. Choose whether to access a scoped reconstruction; deferral persists.
3. Explore ordinary life alongside relevant fragments, preserving item-level locks and missing-media fallbacks.
4. Inspect the actual record, provenance and version limitations; viewing and retaining are separate actions.
5. Compare sources and record a private observation or hypothesis. Conflicting counts and public document changes remain separate until justified by sources.
6. Decide whether to ask someone a question or share a selected finding. Each recipient learns only what was actually delivered.
7. Seek a stronger source before strengthening the conclusion; partial recovery and inconclusive results remain meaningful outcomes.

## Distinctive decisions

A player can understand more than an NPC without secretly granting that NPC knowledge. A recovered private message may change trust without proving criminal responsibility. A newer document may differ from an older one for legitimate reasons. The player can preserve an uncertainty, decline an intrusive action or withhold a personal fragment while continuing an available public route. Core progress should reward supported reasoning, not maximum disclosure or premium access.

## Authority and presentation

Every source should have an explicit availability state and honest limitations. Interface polish cannot manufacture authentication. Recovery percentage describes fragment coverage, not truth probability. A reconstruction cannot send messages as Daniel, access real accounts or establish a live device session. ## Scope of this decision

This document establishes the design role of the existing reconstruction, provenance, comparison and disclosure systems. It does not authorize new recovered contents, missing screenplay, a culprit reveal, Scene 04–18 implementation, new automatic knowledge awards or changes to historical saves. Those integrations still require their approved scene sources.
