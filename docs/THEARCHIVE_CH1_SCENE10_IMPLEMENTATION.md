# Chapter 1 Scene 10 implementation

The master route requires Scene 9 completion and a saved card finding. Home offers manual entry to the fictional public inventory. Search requires an explicit submission of `GH-DSC-03-A`; the result exposes only an index match. Metadata, limitations and comparison are separate actions; one of three provisional conclusions must be explicitly saved privately. No inventory result establishes the document content, custody, authenticity, connection to a person or Warehouse 3.

Returning Home triggers Sophie’s incoming call. Answering is explicit, and declining leaves a callback. Eight decisions each contain three alternatives and require Send; each has a matched Sophie response. The separately described physical warning card bears a fictional number, star and exact warning. It is distinct from Maya’s archive card; its author is not authenticated by Sophie’s description. The warning, final line and ending transition are separate steps. The last line is followed by a three-second pause. The completed scene records the Chapter 1 completion and Chapter 2 unlock event; the chapter menu displays The Other Copy as unlocked but unavailable until separately implemented. No Chapter 2 scenes start. TRACE recovery is unchanged.

The inventory query, inspections, conclusion, call phase, reply drafts, conversation history and ending are persisted in optional `masterScene10` state. A reload during the ending restarts the pause. Scene 10 voice recordings and an approved warning-card photograph were not supplied; this version uses readable subtitles and a text representation. Player messages are scoped to Sophie; no private inventory finding is shared automatically.
